How to play+
01

Break the pattern

Each row is a group of four. Aim for words that have as little in common as possible.

02

Mix things up

Select two words in different rows to swap them. Use Tab and Enter or Space if you’re playing with a keyboard.

03

Go a little further

Check your groups to see your score and each row’s closest pair. Keep swapping to improve. There’s no timer or mistake limit.

What makes two words “related”?+

Words have many meanings, so this game uses one consistent measuring stick: word embeddings. A language model represents each word as a vector. Cosine similarity measures how close two vectors are. Smaller similarities mean greater distance in this model.

Each row has six word pairs. Its relatedness combines their average similarity with an extra penalty for the closest pair. That means one very related pair can still hold a row back. The board score rewards lower total relatedness across all four rows.

The similarities are precomputed, so the game runs entirely in your browser without sending your play to an AI service.

This is a score challenge, not a single secret solution. The model may miss puns, alternate meanings, or connections that seem obvious to you.
